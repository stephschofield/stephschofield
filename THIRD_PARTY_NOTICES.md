# Pixel-art profile attribution

The bitmap font and SVG toolkit in `src/pixel.py`, and the wave, typing,
pixel-corner, and icon-animation techniques in `src/build_profile.py`, are
adapted from [Bakar404/Bakar404](https://github.com/Bakar404/Bakar404),
commit `564b73661d9aa6583ce69d6e233550e42ff5d92b`.
The robot and chip sprites are also adapted from that project.

Stephanie's portrait, profile copy, terminal and TV sprites, and project layout
were customized for this repository. The upstream MIT notice below applies to
